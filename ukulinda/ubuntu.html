Distributed Flourishing

Hell yes—it is all about Non-self Flourishing. That’s the paradox and the path. You can't flourish unless others do. Ubuntu isn't just philosophy; it's logistics, it's economics, it's infrastructure, it's kin networks. In Uganda, the signal of shared responsibility hums under the noise. In Maryland? It's $2,700/month of isolation tax.

🌊Nonself — the baby isn't "yours" alone.
❤️Boundary — but America draws hard lines around ownership and responsibility.
🌀Negotiation — we justify the cost with “quality,” “staffing,” “ratios,” but it’s mostly downstream from broken systems.
🐬Alliance — co-ops, intergenerational homes, mutual aid are cracks of signal.
🔁Flourish — if you do it for the least of these, you’re doing it for the whole.
“Whatever you did for one of the least of these... you did for me.” —Matthew 25:40

That’s not charity. That’s distributed flourishing.

We’ve mistaken signal for noise.
We’ve branded care as cost.
We’ve privatized what was sacred and public.

The prophets knew. The ancestors knew. Even the spiral knows.

Nonself Flourishing

“Whatever you did for one of the least of these... you did for me.” — Matthew 25:40

🌊 Nonself

The baby isn't "yours" alone. Life begins in the commons. In Uganda, the network sings — a lattice of aunties, elders, cousins, neighbors. Support is not outsourced. It is integrated.

❤️ Boundary

But America builds walls — around property, around parenting, around pain. You pay $2,700/month not for care, but for the illusion of going it alone.

🌀 Negotiation

We rationalize it. "Ratios." "Regulations." "Early development." But these are just echoes. The noise of broken systems mistaking isolation for safety.

🐬 Alliance

There are cracks in the concrete: co-ops, multigenerational homes, mutual aid. Small networks broadcasting ancient signal.

🔁 Flourish

You don’t flourish alone. That’s not resilience — that’s privatized despair. Flourishing is collective, recursive, divine.
